# Part 1: Input review

The full table is `inputs.csv`, with one row per input: 98 rows. It is generated by `build_inputs.py`, which reads the
years, sources and fallback counts straight from `model_data/country_inputs.csv`.

Each row gives:
- the name in code, where the input is defined and used (`file:line`), and which displayed numbers it affects;
- what the model needs and what the source measures (publisher, indicator and a quoted definition);
- concept match, currency, fallback and how many countries use it, status, confidence and finding ID.

This file summarises the table and covers the points a row can't hold.

**Scope.**
- Every used column of `country_inputs.csv`: 74 of its 156 columns are read by the model or the app; the other 82
  feed derived columns or nothing.
- Every row of `model_params.csv` (33) and `unit_cost_reference.csv` (13).
- The MAP, IMF, World Bank, WHO and IHME series, scenario constants, MOU schedules, the regression, and every
  hard-coded constant that changes a number.

## The inputs that matter most, in detail

1. **Aid baseline** (`dah_lines.csv`, `prepare_model.py:294-318`). Correct concept, from the current IHME release
   (September 2026). Two problems:
   - **Base years (L-06).** The US cut most of this money in 2025: IHME FGH 2025 says DAH "declined 21% between 2024
     and 2025, driven largely by a 67% drop" in US financing. The model's "Aid at Risk" therefore includes money
     already gone by 2025.
   - **Scope (L-03).** See country selection below.
2. **ART coverage and people living with HIV.** UNAIDS 2026 round, via WHO GHO; 2025 data for 65 of 70 countries.
   Exact and current.
   - The UNAIDS 2026 fact sheet: "41.0 million [37.6-45.0 million] people globally were living with HIV in 2025" and
     "32.1 million ... were accessing antiretroviral therapy".
   - Five countries use a derived fallback (CIV, GTM, IRQ, JOR, NIC).
3. **TB detection rate** (WDI `SH.TBS.DTEC.ZS`). Close match.
   - Notified cases include private-sector notifications, so "covered" means notified, not necessarily treated with
     donor money.
   - The ratio can exceed 100% (L-12).
4. **Malaria effective treatment** (MAP). Exact match to the Lives Saved Tool coverage concept.
   - MAP's 2025 value repeats 2024 (`malaria_inputs.py:32`).
5. **Malaria cases** (MAP Pf + Pv clinical). Modelled, all ages.
   - MAP and WHO differ by more than 30% in 20 of 46 countries (L-23). The largest gap is Pakistan: MAP 14.4M (2025)
     vs WHO 3.15M (2024) vs about 2.7M nationally confirmed in 2023.
6. **Bednet use** (MAP ITN Use, 31 countries).
   - **Under-5 survey fallback (L-07, Minor).** 8 countries use WDI `SH.MLR.NETS.ZS` from a survey since 2015, which
     the World Bank defines as "children under age five sleeping under insecticide-treated bednets". It is applied
     to the whole at-risk population; children under 5 usually have higher use than the population average, so this
     overstates coverage slightly.
   - **Default 50% (L-07).** 31 countries use the 50% default, 22 of them with negligible malaria.
7. **Spraying** (MAP IRS). MAP measures "the proportion of household sprayed", not the share of people (L-21). The
   max() rule credits money-implied spraying in countries where MAP shows none (L-22).
8. **DTP3** (WUENIC via WDI, 2024). One release behind: WUENIC 2025 estimates were published July 2026 (L-17).
9. **Interest burden.**
   - 53 countries use IMF WEO October 2026 net interest (general government); 17 use WDI gross interest (central
     government).
   - Both are standard, but they differ in concept: net vs gross, general vs central (L-34, Minor). In WEO, interest
     equals the primary balance minus the overall balance.
10. **Unit costs.** The weakest sourced inputs (see `assumptions.md`).
    - ART is extrapolated from five southern and eastern African site studies to all 70 countries.
    - TB uses 2014 income-group means.
    - DR-TB costs predate BPaLM (L-16).
    - The 45% above-site markup on ART and TB is cited to PEPFAR 2014 / R4D; I could not find that figure (L-26).

## Country selection

- **Which countries.** The dashboard covers the 71 countries in `country_lists.py` (70 with modelled aid; Mexico has
  none).
- **No documented rule.** On 2026-10-07, 27 countries were removed from a 98-country build list (`prepare_model.py:49`,
  commit `6f31cb3`). The commit message gives no reason, and neither do the README and Methods page.
- **Share of aid covered.**
  - The 71 countries receive **71.5%** of all country-coded HIV, TB, malaria and vaccine DAH: **US$10.35B of
    US$14.48B a year**, 2021-23.
  - **US$2.67B a year (18%) is coded "Global"** (WLD) and US$0.26B "Unallocated".
  - The remaining **US$1.2B** goes to recipients not on the dashboard.

  - Sudan, Afghanistan and Yemen: large vaccine aid and very high child mortality.

- Their omission makes all-country totals smaller than they would be for "all low- and middle-income countries". This
  is right only if every all-country number is labelled "71 countries" (it is on the All Countries page; check
  claims in `dashboard.md`).
- **L-03 (Major, scope).** Document the selection rule. Either add the main missing recipients or state that the
  dashboard covers 71.5% of country-coded disease aid and none of the 18% coded "Global".

**One supply-chain note.**
- WDI and Gapminder series are downloaded from the open-numbers GitHub mirrors (`prepare_model.py:134-137`), not from
  the World Bank API.
- The mirror can lag the World Bank. The years in use match the World Bank's latest for U5MR and TB, but DTP3 is a
  year behind WUENIC.
- Downloading from the World Bank API, or straight from WUENIC, removes that dependency (`data_requests.md` DR-05).
